Stillpoint

A quiet place to name what you're feeling

Settings — Gemini API key

Stillpoint uses your own Gemini API key. Your key is kept only in this browser tab's memory — it is never saved to disk and clears when you close the page.

Don't have a key yet? Get one free from Google AI Studio.

Settings — Local AI mode

Local AI mode runs a small model entirely in this browser tab — nothing is sent anywhere, even the short summary. It requires a browser with WebGPU support and downloads a one-time model file the first time you use it each session.

How are you feeling right now?

Write as much or as little as you'd like. Nothing you type here leaves your device — only a short summary of the emotions themselves does.

Response

Stillpoint keeps your words on your device. Only a short, general summary of your emotions is ever sent — and never during a crisis.
